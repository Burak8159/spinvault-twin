About SpinVault

SpinVault is a student-founded deep-tech project building a storage concept that starts with technical credibility and ends with defensible IP.

Mission

Create a secure storage architecture that uses magnets to control electron flow, enabling reliable and secure data behavior.

Vision

Move from theoretical proof of concept to simulation, physical testing, patent acquisition, licensing, and future production.

How we work

SpinVault is built around a simple operating rule: make every assumption visible before asking anyone to trust the conclusion.

Transparent modeling

We show the simplified equations, inputs, and outputs so technical reviewers can challenge the foundation directly.

Evidence-first communication

We distinguish between concept, simulation, expert feedback, lab plan, and future product claim.

Partner-ready documentation

We translate the technical work into diagrams, validation notes, patent logic, and commercialization milestones.

Company FAQ

Direct answers for partners who want to understand the team and current stage.

Why should a young team be taken seriously?

The team is not asking for blind belief. SpinVault is organizing simulation, external review, competition validation, and lab planning into a reviewable proof path.

What does the team need most?

Advisor feedback, physics mentorship, lab access, and support converting the mechanism into a defensible patent-ready record.

What would make the project fail?

If realistic barrier, material, and spin assumptions cannot produce a useful retention or readout margin, the model should show that early. That honesty is part of the process.
