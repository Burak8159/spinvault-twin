Privacy Notice

This notice explains how this SpinVault prototype handles information. It is written for presentation purposes and should be reviewed by counsel before commercial launch.

Information we collect

This prototype does not send form submissions to a server. If you contact SpinVault outside this site, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as name, email, organization, role, and message.

Local preferences

The website stores theme choice and cookie preference in your browser's local storage so the interface remembers your settings.

How information may be used

Information voluntarily shared with SpinVault may be used to respond to collaboration, advisor, investor, academic, or partnership inquiries.

Analytics and cookies

Optional analytics are not active in this prototype. If analytics are added later, this notice and the cookie settings page should be updated before launch. The cookie settings page lets visitors choose between accepting all optional cookies or keeping only essential storage.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the contact page until a dedicated legal contact address is added.
